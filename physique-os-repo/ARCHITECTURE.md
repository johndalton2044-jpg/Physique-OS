# PHYSIQUE OS — architecture

## Layers
1. **OBSERVATION** (`30-observations`): append-only records `{id,type,date,value,unit,method,source,quality,phaseId,supersedes,correctedBy,retracted,createdAt,flags}`. Corrections supersede; retractions keep the record. Every read takes an as-of date (`_ASOF`) so replay sees only records dated and created on or before that day.
2. **STATE** (`40-models` → `getCurrentState()`): rolling averages, Theil–Sen weight trend with residual noise and confidence, water-noise probability, circumference body composition, waist trend, fat-vs-other split, rate bands by weight zone, goal trajectory, training (e1RM consensus, strength trend, volume, consistency), recovery, appetite, adherence per stream, data trust, muscle-retention risk, step baseline/compensation.
3. **MODEL** (`40-models`): registry `MODELS[]` — class, version, inputs, minimum evidence, assumptions, failure modes. Below minimum evidence every model returns `{status:'insufficient', need:[…]}`, never a number. Energy model path: PRIOR (Mifflin-St Jeor × activity) → BLENDED (w = n/(n+14)) → EMPIRICAL (≥21 logged days at ≥75% coverage) → CALIBRATED (outcome adjustment from ≥3 scored 14-day forecasts).
4. **INDIVIDUALIZATION** (`45-baselines`): what is normal *for this person*. Personal baselines per stream (robust median/MAD, with the last 7 days scored against them), measurement quality per observation (source, flags, protocol adherence, method consistency), change-point detection across weight slope, steps, sleep, intake, fatigue, hunger and e1RM, each with ranked candidate causes and an attribution strength (`supported` / `weakly supported` / `confounded` / `artifact` / `unattributed`). Protocol-change detection marks discontinuities — a new scale, tape, gym, program, exercise, body-fat method or food-database version — which are breaks in comparability, not physiology.
5. **DECISION** (`50-decision`): `diagnose()` ranks findings (stall, slow, rapid, muscle, recovery, underlogging, adherence, compensation, water, rebound) each with evidence, counter-evidence, missing data, ranked causes and a confirming test. `decide()` walks the priority lattice — safety › data integrity › active experiment › adherence › rate of loss › performance › plateau › nutrition › training › activity › optimization — and returns one action with WHY / DO / REVERSE-IF / confidence / recheck / trace. Hold steady is a first-class result. Interventions become experiments with a stamped prediction; unsupported outcomes become negative knowledge that the lattice consults.

6. **RESPONSE + UNCERTAINTY** (`52-response`): uncertainty propagates from intake coverage → TDEE interval → energy-balance interval, and the resulting ratio caps decision confidence (a deficit smaller than its own interval cannot carry a numeric decision). The personal response matrix learns effect-per-unit from this record's own completed experiments and clean interventions; exercise-level response reads per-lift e1RM slope against that lift's own noise. `counterfactual()` projects a proposed change without mutating anything. Value of information is scored as uncertainty reduction × decision impact − burden, with a time-to-answer. Predictions are stamped with their context (phase, weight zone, trust, model version) and calibration is kept per context.
7. **SCHEDULER + CONTRACTS** (`92-scheduler`): an offline app has no cron, so jobs run on open and record what they did — forecast stamping and scoring, experiment evaluation, data-quality sweep, migration check, backup restore dry-run, update check, food-database version check. `dataContractIssues()` asserts that every observation type reaches a real consumer, every model input resolves, and every model declares class, assumptions, failure conditions, uncertainty and consumers. The device-test checklist records what a headless gate cannot prove.

## Nutrient basis (schema 2)
Every food declares what its numbers are stated per: `g` (per 100 g), `ml` (per 100 mL — USDA Branded products whose label serving unit is millilitres), or `serving` (recipes with volume ingredients and no known mass). USDA Branded `foodNutrients` are **per 100 g or per 100 mL**, never per serving. Grams are never derived from millilitres without a stated density: a mL-basis product is logged in mL, fl oz or servings, and `logFood` refuses a gram quantity for one. Every logged entry records its quantity, basis, and whether the conversion was approximate.

## Nothing is destroyed, nothing is rewritten
Corrections supersede, deletions retract, edited food entries are superseded. Every suppression stores the date
it became known (`correctedAt`, `retractedAt`, `supersededAt`), and visibility is a question about a moment in
time (`_visible`, `_sessionVisible`, `_foodLogVisible`) rather than a boolean read of present state. Using the
flags directly would let a correction made today erase what the system knew last month, which is precisely
what replay exists to prevent.

## One primitive per mutable thing
`updatePhase(id, patch, opts)` is the only path that changes a phase target: it captures pre-mutation values,
always records phase history so `phaseAsOf()` can reconstruct the phase on any past day, and generates exactly
one intervention. `setProgram(key, opts)` is the only path that changes the training program. Two mutation
paths meant two behaviours — one recorded history and one did not, and both created interventions, producing
duplicates whose `from` value was read after the mutation had already happened.

## Knowledge dates vs effective dates
An effective date says when something applied; a creation date says when the system learned of it. Replay uses the latter. A phase, experiment, decision, negative or intervention created today is invisible to a replay of last month even if its effective date falls inside that window, and phase target edits are reverted to what they were on the replay day. Without this, a replay marks its own homework.

## Navigation as a specification
`94-navigation.js` holds the command taxonomy. The command palette, the keyboard map, the utility rail and the
interaction matrix are all projections of it, so a capability cannot be reachable one way and invisible
another. `interactionMatrix()` fails the self-test if any command is reachable only by keyboard or gesture,
and the gate additionally asserts that every `data-act` rendered in the interface — and every action
dispatched indirectly from the attention queue, missing-data report, data-quality report, setup checklist,
timeline and search — resolves to a registered action.

## Invariants the self-test enforces
Schema validation and migration; correction/retraction semantics; sum-vs-mean aggregation and manual-over-food-log precedence; undo; statistics; e1RM; the 14 scenario fixtures and their expected decisions; epistemic rendering (predictive → lavender, measured never inferred); model registry completeness; decision field completeness; provenance + age on tiles; ledger stamped before outcome and immutable while pending; replay leakage = 0 and no mutation; hold steady on good data; snapshots/archive; negative knowledge redirecting a decision; an active experiment freezing lower levels; food search/scaling/snapshot immutability/retraction; the temporal ledger (a later correction, retraction or food-log edit never changes what a past day knew, in both directions); one intervention per phase edit stamped with the pre-mutation value; program history including what a change moved away from; the interaction matrix and indirect action resolution; branded-food basis certification (gram and millilitre products, refused gram conversions, missing energy staying null rather than zero); schema-1 → 2 migration with rejection of newer, sub-minimum and non-integer versions; replay knowledge-date visibility; personal baselines, measurement quality and off-protocol exclusion; change-point attribution including measurement artifacts; uncertainty propagation and its confidence ceiling; graded experiment conclusions (a confounded experiment creates no negative knowledge); projections never mutating the record; data and model contracts; scheduler jobs; adapter honesty when data is not installed; evidence-registry citations; exercise ontology; reference provenance; demo determinism and marking; every `data-act` registered; 12 views on a fixture and on an empty record without quarantined errors; keyboard focusability; every command runnable; accessibility audit; exports.

## Storage
`DB` is the single in-memory truth. `save()` bumps `revision`, mirrors to localStorage synchronously, queues IndexedDB (`physique-os/kv/db`). Boot paints from the mirror, then adopts the IndexedDB copy if newer. Daily auto-backups are written to IndexedDB; other tabs are detected through the storage event. Corrupt data is preserved raw and a fresh record is started with a corruption event in the ledger.

## Build
`node build.mjs` concatenates `src/` into `dist/index.html` (region-marked, no bundler, `eval`/`new Function`/`document.write` forbidden and asserted), writes `sw.js`, the manifest, icons and checksums. `node tests/run.mjs` boots the built file in jsdom, exercises views, sheets, dispatcher, palette, keyboard, persistence and exports, and runs the in-app self-test; non-zero exit on any failure. `scripts/food-build.mjs` streams the USDA Branded zip (never extracted) into positional shards with a delta-encoded inverted index and a GTIN index; raw USDA files are build inputs only.
