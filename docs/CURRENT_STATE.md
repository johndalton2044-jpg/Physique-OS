## Known limitations

- **Two records of one food that disagree, settled by a declared rule** (surfaced): the nutrients that differ are named with both values; each record is checked against its own macros (4/4/9, the food sheet’s own check); the first of your own entry, USDA laboratory analysis, the USDA branded label and Open Food Facts that agrees with itself is used, and the other is kept visible. Scanning a barcode you already have an entry for offers the record the rule chooses, and the food sheet says why. The order and thresholds are a heuristic contract, not validated.
- **Protein across the day’s meals, the daily total first** (surfaced): about 0.4 g/kg per meal across four meals once the day reaches 1.6 g/kg: a day short of its total is told to reach the total first; a day that is enough is told how many meals reach the amount and which to add to. On the Food tab.
- **One plan authority (every plan change through changePlan, one version each)** (integrated).
- **Encrypted sync between devices** (integrated): needs a persistent disk; production mode refuses to start without one and without off-host backups.
- **Verified off-host server backups, restore and health** (integrated): tested against a mock S3 that checks signatures and payload hashes; not yet against a real provider.
- **Adaptive plan** (surfaced).
- **Personal response model** (surfaced): Hierarchical (Stage E): the phase contexts of a response are pooled toward the person’s own estimate through the shared Bayes engine once two contexts have their own data, and the strength trends of trained regions are pooled the same way before one is called lagging. Partial pooling as in Gelman et al., Bayesian Data Analysis (3rd ed., ch. 5)..
- **Forecasts that compete for accuracy** (surfaced).
- **Unified optimiser** (surfaced): a deeper calorie cut is credited with the everyday movement it is expected to cost: the personal NEAT response (steps a day per 10% deficit, Bayesian from a population prior), applied only to the share of the calorie effect still resting on the population figure; each option simulated forward 8 weeks (simulatePolicies): the forecast competition’s winning model for the path with no change, plus each change’s effect counted only if carried out, with an 80% interval; the plan records the simulated outcome when an option is chosen.
- **Marginal returns: the next unit of each dose** (surfaced): population curves; the sets curve becomes personal (fitted across regions, Bayesian) once the person’s data carry a fifth of it.
- **Goal conflicts and arbitration on seven dimensions** (surfaced): declared rules with heuristic contracts; each conflict reports its trade-off, the chosen objective or none, and the expected consequence; the person chooses which goal leads.
- **Body composition: fat and lean trajectories** (surfaced): the fat-mass rate from the weight trend's partition prior and each body-fat method's own trend (weighted by that method's error, methods never mixed), lean as the remainder; masses only when a measured reading from the last 60 days anchors them.
- **Aerobic fitness: VO2max level and trend** (surfaced): per modality family, from steady sessions with heart rate and pace or power (a Kalman filter for the level, its intervals scored against the next session); the trend a weighted regression with a prior centred on no change; capacity sits beside the cardio minutes in the capability vector; its 80% interval calibrated by its own one-step-ahead record (self-calibration).
- **Mobility, conditioning, power and speed: field tests and their responses** (surfaced): five field tests in the quick log (sit-and-reach, knee-to-wall, one-minute heart-rate recovery, countermovement jump, 20 m sprint), each related to its dose (mobility minutes, cardio minutes, lower-body sets) averaged over the four weeks before the test: a population prior per unit of dose updated by the person's own slope through bayesUpdate, with the personal weight; in the capability vector and the physiology card.
- **Causal estimates of each change: interrupted series and matched periods** (surfaced): each Response's before/after estimate is reported beside an interrupted series (level and rate against the old trend projected to the change, standard errors widened for day-to-day carry-over) and a matched-periods estimate (the same comparison at dates in the person's own record with no change: same weekday and phase, a similar starting trend, Student's t for few periods); a person's deliberate changes, pooled per unit, are an identification strategy of their own. Observational: it rules out drift and regression to the mean, not a cause that arrived with the change; and the day-by-day path without the change (responseCounterfactual): the trend before continued, with what matched periods did on their own where they exist, an 80% band, shown with a chart in each Response’s sheet.
- **Personal knowledge: versions, conflicts and decay** (surfaced): each Response carries its version and the method version that derived it, and is derived again (a new version naming the one it replaced) when it matures or the method changes; negative knowledge carries its method version and its place among findings about the same change; findings that disagree (two responses beyond their noise, or 'did not work' beside a clear response) are shown side by side with their age weights; in the personal response model each response counts for half as much every 270 days.
- **Experiment portfolio: the next test by expected information gain** (surfaced): every lever's ready-made test on one scale: the expected information gain 1/2 ln(1 + s0^2/se^2), with s0 the lever's SD now (widened by half of any disagreement between its findings) and se the outcome's noise over a Response's windows over the dose likely carried out (the person's own response errors where measured over most of both windows); ranked per week of testing; a test that would narrow the estimate by less than 10% is not worth running.
- **Sensor fusion: one value from several sources** (surfaced): a measured quantity from two or more sources (told apart by provider or device) is fused on every day: each source put on the reference source’s scale (your preferred one, else the one with the most readings) by its mean same-day difference, each reading weighted by its quality over its source’s measured noise squared, a reading the others contradict set aside when there are three; from readings known by the date asked about only; body fat left to its own reconciliation.
- **User-approved automation: approval policy and audit** (surfaced): every automated action has a level: view (changes nothing) runs; routine (an undoable record) runs under the standing approval recorded when its rule is turned on; important (the plan, targets, programme, profile, any deletion) never runs on a trigger, only holds a proposal that applies when approved, through changePlan; an undeclared action is important; every decision is in the audit log.
- **Self-calibration of every interval** (surfaced): the forecast competition’s calibration made the one rule for every model that makes interval predictions (weight and strength forecasts, aerobic capacity, personal response predictions): the factor that would have made the 80% interval hold 80% of the time, learned on older predictions and checked on newer ones, widening or narrowing the live interval once ten predictions are scored; one report says whether each model’s intervals are honest; and execution probabilities: a backtest (fitted on the older half of the days, scored on the newer half, Brier score against the base rate) and a prospective record (each day’s planned items stamped before the day, scored after it).
